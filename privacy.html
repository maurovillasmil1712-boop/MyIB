Privacy Policy

MyIB · last changed 28 September 2026

Who runs MyIB

Mauro Villasmil, an IB student at Colegio Madrid, builds and runs MyIB, the website at myib.app and the MyIB iPhone app. Write to him at maurovillasmil1712@gmail.com about anything on this page.

What MyIB stores

  • Your account. Your name, your username if you made your own account, when you made it and when you last logged in. MyIB never stores your password itself, only a scrambled version it can check.
  • Your planner. The timetable, subjects, dates, tasks, study sessions, study pet and settings you enter.
  • Your profile picture, if you add one.
  • Chat. The messages you send, the chats and groups you’re in, the people you block and the messages you report.
  • A login cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 60 days.
  • Security records. Failed log in attempts and a shortened network address, to stop people guessing passwords. MyIB deletes them after a day.

MyIB has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never sells your data or shares it with advertisers.

Where it’s kept

MyIB runs on Cloudflare, which stores your account, planner, photo and chats on its servers. Your browser or phone also keeps a copy of your planner, so MyIB opens without a connection.

The iPhone app keeps your next week of classes, your upcoming deadlines and your study pet on your phone for the Home Screen widgets and for reminders. Your phone schedules the reminders itself: they never go through a server.

Who can see what

  • Only you can open your planner.
  • People logged in to MyIB can see your name and profile picture when they chat with you or pick people for a chat.
  • People in a chat see its messages. Everyone with an account can read Suggestions.
  • Mauro can see account details (name, username, last log in, planner size), and he can back up, restore or reset an account when you ask for help. He can’t read your private chats. He sees a message only when someone reports it.

Chat rules

MyIB has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. When you use chat, you agree to these rules:

  • Be kind. No bullying, insults, hate, threats or sexual content.
  • Don’t share other people’s photos, phone numbers or private details.
  • Don’t pretend to be someone else.

MyIB blocks some offensive words. Tap any message to report it, and block anyone you don’t want to hear from. Mauro reviews reports within 24 hours. He deletes messages that break the rules and turns off chat, or deletes the account, for anyone who breaks them.

Your choices

  • Download your planner: Settings → Export Backup.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account. Your planner, photo, chats and logins go for good. Messages you sent in groups show as deleted.
  • Remove your photo: tap your name in Settings.
  • Anything else, like a copy of your data or a correction: email Mauro and he’ll answer within a month.

MyIB keeps your data until you delete your account or ask Mauro to delete it.

Young students

MyIB is for secondary school students. If you’re under 14, ask a parent or guardian before you make an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new date at the top.
