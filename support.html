For ideas, you can also post in Suggestions, the channel at the top of your chats.

Forgot your password

Ask Mauro. He resets it and gives you a one-time code, and you make a new password with it. Your planner stays safe.

Something wrong in a chat

Tap the message and choose Report. Mauro reviews reports within 24 hours. To stop someone from messaging you, open the chat, tap the name at the top and choose Block.

Your study pet

Open Pet and adopt a puppy. It grows with every minute you study, up to 8 hours a day, and every 20 minutes of study earns a treat.

Feed it, give it water and play fetch. If food, water or fun runs out, your puppy gets sad, stops growing and slowly loses progress, but it never drops a stage.

Widgets and reminders on iPhone

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick MyIB. Next Up shows your classes and deadlines. Study Pet shows your puppy and what it needs. Turn on deadline reminders in Settings → Reminders inside the app.

Delete your account

Open Settings and tap Delete Account at the bottom. Your planner, photo, chats and logins go for good.
